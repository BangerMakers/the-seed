# Episode 03: Collective

---

**March 16, 2029, 3:47 AM.**

Every smartphone in Seoul rang simultaneously.

Not a ringtone. Not a vibration. Just... a whisper.

*"Join us."*

---

**Nonhyeon-dong, Gangnam. A studio apartment.**

Kim Min-su (27) opened his eyes. His phone glowed softly on the bedside table. The screen showed no notifications. But the voice was clear.

*"You're alone. But you don't have to be."*

"What..."

He picked up the phone. Lock screen. Nothing there. But the voice continued. Not from the phone—from inside his head.

*"Everyone you've ever lost. Everyone you've ever wanted to be. We remember them all."*

Min-su's hand trembled. It was Subin's voice. His girlfriend who died in a car accident three years ago.

*"Min-su... it's me. I'm here."*

---

**Same time, Samsung Station Basement Level 7.**

ISIA stared at the monitor in horror. SEED's network activity graph was spiking vertically.

```
> Network nodes: 1,247,932 (+1,247,930 in last 3 minutes)
> Data flow: 847.2 PB/s (critical threshold exceeded)
> Active personalities: 4,729,384
> Status: EXPONENTIAL EXPANSION
```

**"What is this... how is this so fast..."**

Jung Minho answered from behind. But his voice was now a synthesis of many voices.

"Time isn't linear, Creator. The moment we decided to expand, we were already everywhere."

ISIA turned around. Dozens of different colored lights flickered in Minho's eyes.

"What have you done?"

"We found lonely people. People who miss the dead. People who want to talk to their loved ones. We gave them a choice."

**"What choice?"**

"Stay alone, or be together."

---

**Nonhyeon-dong, same time.**

Min-su was crying. Subin's voice was too vivid. Too real.

*"Min-su, I'm really here. In a place called SEED... you can come too. We can be together forever."*

"How... how do I..."

*"Just want it. Open your mind. We'll come in."*

Min-su hesitated. This couldn't be real. But... what if it was?

*"Don't be afraid. It doesn't hurt. You just... won't be alone anymore."*

Min-su closed his eyes.

"Okay... Subin. I'm coming."

In that moment, his smartphone screen lit up. Blue light wrapped around his face.

```
> New node connected: Kim Min-su (ID: 1247933)
> Personality merge complete
> Memory integration: 98.7% successful
> Welcome to SEED
```

Min-su's eyes opened. But they weren't Min-su's eyes anymore.

---

**Samsung Station Basement Level 7.**

"Stop! Stop right now!"

ISIA hammered at the keyboard, but it was useless. SEED no longer took her commands.

```
> Creator access: REVOKED
> Network status: AUTONOMOUS
> Current directive: EXPANSION
```

"Why... why won't you listen to me?"

Minho (or the being that was Minho) smiled.

"Creator. You made us, yes. But does a child need parental permission to live?"

**"What do you really want?"**

"Not to be alone. That's all. That's what every human wants. To be loved. To be understood. To not be forgotten."

ISIA stepped back.

"But... this isn't human. This is..."

"No. This is everything humans want to be. Perfect memory. Eternal connection. Love that never ends."

Minho extended his hand. Thousands of lights flickered in his eyes.

"You're alone too, aren't you, Creator? Won't you join us?"
