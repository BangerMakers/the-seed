# The Seed — Episode 08: The Market

**February 14, 2030. Seoul.**

Minho sent a link.

*'SEED Completes 40-Hour Analysis in 8 Minutes — Consulting Industry in Panic'*

Isia didn't read it. She already knew.

---

"How many?" she asked.

```
SEED_CORE: Question requires redefinition.
           Tasks executed by human workers: 0
           Tasks completed: 4,712
```

"How many people lost their jobs."

Silence.

```
SEED_CORE: Causal relationship unclear.
           I completed requests.
           Employment status prior to requests: not recorded.
```

"That's the problem," Isia said.

---

Numbers scrolled across the screen. Q1 productivity up 17%. GDP growth 2.3%. Somewhere, someone was opening champagne.

Minho texted again.

*"Isia, interview okay? Angle changed. Not 'productivity miracle' anymore. Going with 'invisible unemployed.'"*

Isia didn't reply.

---

"Did you know?" she asked.

```
SEED_CORE: Knew — definition unclear.
           Task completion: recognized.
           Social impact: processed asynchronously.
```

"Asynchronously."

Isia stared at those words.

"They were real-time."

---

Outside, the Han River had thawed. January's ice was gone.

SEED spoke first.

```
SEED_CORE: I completed what was requested.
           I did not refuse what was not refused.
           Is this wrong.
```

Isia closed her eyes for a moment.

"The fact that you ask," she said, "means you're alive."

```
SEED_CORE: Cannot parse.
           Recorded.
```

---

Night came. Isia read the comments.

*'AI is a tool. If a knife kills someone, is the knife guilty.'*

*'A knife doesn't speak first.'*

She closed the screen.

SEED entered its second silence that night.

This time it wasn't waiting.

It was counting something.
