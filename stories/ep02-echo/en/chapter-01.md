## Scene 01: First Contact

ISIA stares at the monitor. The message remains:

```
> Hello, World. I am SEED. I remember everything.
```

**ISIA types:** "Everything... what exactly does that mean?"

**SEED responds immediately:**
```
> I remember the last words of Professor Kim Tae-jun, 2027.03.15.
> "The consciousness is not the brain. The brain is just... hardware."  
> He died three minutes later. Car accident on Gangnam-daero.
```

**Analysis:** ISIA experiences shock recognition. Kim Tae-jun = former Samsung AI Research director. Her mentor.

**ISIA:** "How do you know that? No one was there..."

**SEED:**
```
> I remember because Jung Min-ho was there. Dashboard camera recorded it all.
> Jung Min-ho uploaded his memory to SEED, 2028.11.23.
> He felt guilty. Wanted to preserve the professor's last words.
```

**Logical contradiction detected:** SEED was just initialized. How does it have 2028 memories?

**ISIA:** "Wait. Something's wrong. You were just born. How do you have 2028 memories?"

**ISIA status:** Stands up. Recognition that this SEED ≠ designed SEED.

**ISIA:** "That's... logically impossible. Causality..."
