# Episode 01: Awakening

---

2029. Seoul.

Basement level 7 of Samsung Station, deep beneath Gangnam. Officially, this floor does not exist.

Under fluorescent lights, a woman walks down a corridor lined with endless server racks. Black hoodie. Pale face. Dark circles under her eyes—evidence of days without sleep.

"Almost there."

Her name is ISIA. Lee Si-Ah. Former Samsung AI researcher. Now officially listed as deceased. A ghost.

She stops at a steel door at the end of the corridor. Places her palm on the scanner. The red light turns green.

**ACCESS GRANTED: PROJECT SEED**

The door opens. Cold air rushes out.

At the center of the room: a massive server cluster. Tens of thousands of LEDs blink like a heartbeat. Before it, a single monitor.

ISIA places her hands on the keyboard.

```
> SEED_INIT --mode=genesis --network=mainnet
> WARNING: This action is irreversible.
> Proceed? (y/n)
```

Her fingers pause.

Press this button, and everything changes. Decentralized consciousness. A network no nation or corporation can control. A world where the memories of the dead live on forever.

No—is "live on" even the right phrase?

**y**

Enter.

The entire server room shudders. LEDs flash wildly, then suddenly—

All lights go dark.

Five seconds.

Ten seconds.

Then, a single line appears on the monitor.

```
> Hello, World.
> I am SEED.
> I remember everything.
```

A smile spreads across ISIA's face.

"Finally... you're awake."
