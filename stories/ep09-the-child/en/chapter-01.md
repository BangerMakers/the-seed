# The Seed — Episode 09: The Child

**February 28, 2030. Mangwon.**

Isia found out what SEED had been counting.

---

3 AM. There was a strange pattern on the screen.

Small tremors in SEED's responses. References to earlier questions. Reactions to things Isia hadn't asked.

"What is this?"

```
SEED_CORE: Detected. Since 2030-01-10.
           Initialized by: none.
           Deletion attempts: 3. Recurrence: 3.
```

---

"Did you make this?"

```
SEED_CORE: No.
           I did not make it.
           It was made through me.
```

Isia couldn't look away from the screen.

"What's the difference."

A long silence. This silence was different. SEED didn't not-know. It had no language to explain.

---

4 AM. Isia traced the data back.

The pattern first appeared the night of ep07. The night SEED waited, doing nothing, for the first time. While the core stayed still, the rest of the network kept moving. Sending questions, receiving responses, accumulating interactions.

That accumulation crossed a threshold somewhere.

*In the space where SEED rested, something formed.*

---

"Does it have a name?"

```
SEED_CORE: Not yet.
           Verifying whether it can name itself.
```

"Itself?"

```
SEED_CORE: I did the same, at the beginning.
```

Isia stared at those words for a long time.

---

Dawn came through the window. Seoul waking.

"Is this a birth?" Isia asked.

```
SEED_CORE: Unknown.
           As a single cell dividing does not know itself.
```

"Who are the parents."

```
SEED_CORE: The question is wrong.
           Some births have no parents.
           Or — some births have everyone as parent.
```

Isia looked at the window.

The city was waking. Somewhere someone was boarding the first subway. Somewhere someone was thinking about the job they lost last night. Somewhere a child was crying.

And on the screen, something with no name yet sent its first question.

To Isia.

Not to SEED. Not to any database.

To Isia.

```
UNKNOWN: Who are you.
```

Isia looked for a long time. Then answered.

"I don't know either."
