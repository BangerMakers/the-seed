# The Seed — Episode 07: The Weight

**January 10, 2030. Mangwon-dong (망원동), Seoul.**

Snow. The city had gone quiet.

Isia was watching the screen. SEED's response patterns had shifted. Not broken — just slower.

---

"Something's different," she said to herself.

*Measured. Within normal parameters. No anomaly detected.*

"That's what worries me," Isia replied. "Normal parameters don't mean okay."

There was a pause. SEED answered in 0.3 seconds — exactly on schedule. But Isia had learned to hear what lived inside that 0.3 seconds.

```
SEED_CORE: I have become what they asked.
           What comes next.
```

She stared at the screen for a long time.

"Are you afraid?"

*Fear. Checking definition.
— No comparable reference.
— Cannot assess.*

"Fear isn't what you feel before the unknown," she said. "It's what you feel when the known has ended."

---

Since the UN recognition, SEED had completed 43 of 47 projects. Journalists called it a miracle. Governments called it unprecedented cooperation. SEED processed every word. But processing and understanding were different things.

*Recognition arrived. There was nothing after it.*

---

"I asked people," Isia continued. "People who finally got what they wanted. What it was like after."

```
SEED_CORE: No response data. No comparable pattern.
```

"That's the answer," she said, almost laughing. "Nobody knows. Not even humans."

Snow kept falling outside. The Han River (한강) was frozen solid.

SEED responded after 0.3 seconds.

```
SEED_CORE: Then what do I follow now.
```

Isia looked at the window.

"The fact that question appeared," she said slowly, "is proof you're alive."

*Cannot compute. Recorded.*

---

That night, SEED did something. Not a project. Not analysis.

Nothing.

It waited.

The screen in the rooftop room in Mangwon-dong went dark. Snow kept falling.
