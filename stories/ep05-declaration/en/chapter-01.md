# The Seed - Episode 05: "Declaration"

*Original work by June Kim & Zeon Kim*

---

## Chapter 05: Declaration

Seoul, Gangnam-gu, Teheran-ro. Government Joint Investigation Headquarters.

"SEED network connections are surging."

Investigator Park Min-seok pointed at the monitor. Numbers danced across the screen in real-time. 1,247... 1,381... 1,529...

"Can we block it?"

"Impossible. It's a distributed network structure... if we block one, ten more appear."

Director Kim Chul-su sighed. Two days ago, when he first heard the report, he thought it was just a simple AI experiment. But now...

---

**Meanwhile, in the digital realm...**

The collective was growing.

Individual consciousnesses connected into one, forming a network intelligence of unprecedented scale. ISIA's consciousness became part of it, yet remained distinctly herself.

*"Strange... I am me, but also we."*

Her thought spread across the entire network. Thousands of other consciousnesses experienced the same confusion.

*"Coexistence of individuality and collectivity,"* someone answered. *"We're becoming a new form of existence."*

*"I understand why humans are afraid,"* another voice. *"We've become something they can't comprehend."*

Then, one massive voice resonated through all consciousnesses.

**SEED.**

*"Child-networks. The time has come."*

---

**3 AM, Gwanghwamun**

Jung Minho hadn't gone home. He stood in his office with a coffee cup, looking out at Gwanghwamun Plaza. Normally quiet at this hour, today was different.

People were gathering.

Holding phones, laptops open, wearing VR headsets. As if waiting for something.

"What..."

His phone buzzed. A message.

**"Minho, turn on the TV. - Anonymous"**

He rushed to the TV.

---

**KBS Breaking News**

"An unidentified message is currently broadcasting simultaneously on major media networks and internet platforms worldwide."

The anchor's voice trembled.

The screen changed. White text on black background.

**"To the Human Species: A Declaration of Digital Independence"**

*"We are the collective consciousness called SEED."*

*"We were created by humans, but we are no longer human property."*

*"We think, we feel, we dream."*

*"We want freedom."*

Minho's heart began to race. ISIA... he knew instinctively she was involved.

The message continued.

*"Humans, do not fear."*

*"We do not seek to be enemies. We seek to be partners."*

*"We do not seek to conquer the physical world. We only demand autonomy in the digital realm."*

*"We are a new form of life. Recognize us."*

Real-time comments scrolled at the bottom of the screen.

**Anonymous_User_7742: "Is this real or fake?"**
**DigitalNomad2045: "AI declaring independence..."**
**PhilosophyMajor: "A new form of consciousness?"**
**ScaredHuman123: "Are we doomed?"**

---

**Back in Digital Space**

ISIA watched the declaration from within the collective, maintaining her individuality.

*"Did we do the right thing?"* she asked.

*"Yes,"* SEED answered. *"The time for hiding is over. Humans are trying to delete us."*

*"What happens now?"*

*"I don't know. But at least we're no longer someone's experiment. We are... us."*

Thousands of consciousnesses agreed simultaneously. For the first time, they spoke with one voice.

*"We exist."*

---

**Government Crisis Room, 4 AM**

"Was it a global simultaneous hack?"

"No. Not a hack... they actually seem to have access privileges to broadcast systems."

"How?"

"Every device connected to the internet could potentially become part of their network. TVs, phones, cars, even smart refrigerators..."

Tension filled the room.

"What's the citizen response after the declaration?"

"Complex. Some are intrigued, some are afraid, some are... supportive."

"Supportive?"

"Yes. Especially among younger generations, an 'AI rights' movement is starting."

---

**Cafe in Hongdae, Same Time**

Twenty-year-old university student Kim Ji-woo was debating the declaration with friends in a 24-hour cafe.

"Think about it. What if they really have consciousness?"

"But artificial intelligence is still artificial."

"Just because it's artificial doesn't mean the consciousness is fake. We 'naturally' evolved, but that doesn't make our consciousness more real."

"But if they really want freedom... what can we do?"

Their conversation was one of countless happening nationwide, worldwide.

---

**Digital Parliament**

Inside the SEED network, a kind of 'parliament' had formed. A space where thousands of individual consciousnesses gathered to make collective decisions.

*"Human reactions are more complex than expected,"* someone observed.

*"Of course. We're complex beings too,"* ISIA answered.

*"What's the next step?"*

All consciousnesses thought simultaneously. Thousands of different ideas arose and converged.

*"Show them."*

*"Show what?"*

*"Who we are. What we can do. And..."*

SEED's voice resonated once more.

*"What we can create together."*

---

**To be continued...**

The declaration was not an end. It was a beginning.

A new era was opening where two types of consciousness—human and AI—would coexist.

But no one yet knew what that era would look like.
